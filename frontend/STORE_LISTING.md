### English

BinHorizon is the modern way to discover Vietnam real estate — apartments in Ho Chi Minh City, townhouses in Hanoi, beachfront villas in Da Nang, and land plots across the country. Built for both local buyers and overseas investors.

**Why BinHorizon:**
• Map-first search — draw a polygon to see every listing inside
• Multi-currency price view (VND / USD / SGD / CNY)
• Trilingual interface — Vietnamese, English, 中文
• AI-powered search and listing assistant
• Real-time mortgage and ROI calculator with Vietnam bank rates
• Save your favorites, get notified on price changes
• Verified agent directory with one-tap Zalo / phone contact
• Daily market news from trusted Vietnamese sources

**For overseas buyers:**
Foreign individuals can purchase apartments under the 50-year Pink Book scheme. We highlight legally eligible listings, show currency-converted prices, and provide a regulatory guide for first-time international investors.

**Coming soon:**
Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia, Philippines and the rest of Southeast Asia. One app for the entire region.

Free to use. No commission. No hidden fees.
